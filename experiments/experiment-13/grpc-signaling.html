Experiment 13 gRPC Signaling Diagrams — Authorization Stack

Arrowhead 5 Evolved · Two gRPC interfaces with distinct roles — one is a PDP–PEP decision channel, the other is a CA-to-PIP attribute provisioning stream. See grpc-summary.html for analysis.

Diagram 1 — AuthorizationPDP.Decide

A canonical PDP–PEP interaction (with deviations). DynamicOrchestration acts as a control-plane PEP: it calls authz-pdp once per provider candidate and includes only PERMIT results in the orchestration response. The PEP is at the service-discovery layer, not the data plane.

Diagram 2 — CertificateLifecycle.Subscribe

Not a PDP–PEP interface. profile-ca streams certificate lifecycle events to PIP, which pre-populates a SubjectStore with cert-valid and cert-level attributes. This is the attribute provisioning pipeline that feeds Diagram 1's XACML decisions.

How they connect: Diagram 2 profile-ca ──stream──▶ PIP SubjectStore (cert-valid, cert-level as XACML subject attrs) ↓ Diagram 1 authz-pdp evaluates those attrs in every Decide() call Revocation in Diagram 2 propagates immediately to Diagram 1 decisions for that subject.
